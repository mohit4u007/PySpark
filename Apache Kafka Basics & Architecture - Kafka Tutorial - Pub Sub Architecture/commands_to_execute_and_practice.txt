Publisher and Subscribe distributed streaming service or messaging system architecture
Producer and Consumer
Msg can only be published for a particular topic
Kafka works in a cluster fashion
Server or nodes are known as Brockers and Zookeeper maintains the metadata and manages the brockers
Brockers and also known as bootstrap servers
Brockers maitain metadata of topic among themselves
Horizonal scaling in kafka cluster
Kafka topic is distributed in form of partitions, FIFO format
Offset is maintained at the zookeeper so that the consumer doesn't loose the messages it has already processed
Replication factor in kafka for fault tolerance, same msg is replicated in multiple brokers, of which one is a leader and rest follower
